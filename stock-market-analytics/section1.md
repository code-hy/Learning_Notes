## 6. Data-Driven Investment Decisions & Macroeconomic Frameworks

### Key Macroeconomic Indicators
- **Gross Domestic Product (GDP):** Tracked via FRED (`pandas_datareader` code `GDP`) [15]. Long-term US GDP growth target band is 2%–3% per year [16]. Normal trading strategies apply within this range; values outside warrant adjusting growth or contraction expectations [16].
- **Inflation & Consumer Price Index (CPI):** US CPI is around 2.5%, down from post-COVID peaks of 7–8% [17]. Cash left in standard bank savings accounts often yields returns below inflation, resulting in a loss of real purchasing power [18].
- **Interest Rates & Risk-Free Rates:** 
  - *Federal Funds Rate:* The rate at which banks trade overnight funds (`FEDFUNDS` on FRED), currently sitting at ~3.63% [20].
  - *Risk-Free Rate:* Proxied by the 3-month US Treasury Bill rate (~4%), which serves as a baseline discount factor for future cash flow valuations [21].

### Cash Management Ladder
- **Hold Cash:** 0% return; constant real value loss due to inflation [18].
- **Bank Savings Accounts:** Low yield; guaranteed by government protection schemes up to limits, but typically below inflation [18].
- **Money Service Businesses (e.g., Wise):** Slightly higher yields derived from global money operations [18, 19].
- **Uninvested Broker Cash:** Programs via brokers (e.g., Trade Republic, Interactive Brokers) offering higher competitive yields on cash balances [19].

### Asset Classes & Benchmark Expectations
- **Bonds (Government & Corporate):** Yields range between 4%–6%, offering 2–3x higher returns than traditional bank accounts with moderate risk [21, 22].
- **S&P 500 Index:** Primary benchmark tracking 500 leading US companies [22]. Historical average annual return is ~10% over 80+ years (~7% net of inflation) [23]. Subject to significant market drawdowns (e.g., 2008, 2022 drops of 10–30%+) [23].
- **Conservative Sweet Spot:** For stock-focused investment strategies, target a net annual return of 6%–10% net of inflation [26].
- **Institutional Benchmarks:** Warren Buffett achieved ~20% annual returns over decades; Renaissance Technologies reported ~60% gross annual returns [26, 27].

## 7. Technical Environment & Development Setup

### Google Colab (Primary Platform)
- **Advantages:** Zero local environment setup, free access to cloud resources including GPUs/TPUs, pre-installed Python libraries, built-in Gemini AI assistance, and interactive Plotly visualizations [29].
- **Limitations:** Session timeouts, need to re-upload local data files per session, and usage limits on free tiers [30].

### Alternatives
- **Local Jupyter / Anaconda:** Full local control without session disconnects, but performance depends on local machine specs [30].
- **IDE Setup (VS Code / PyCharm):** Recommended for hybrid workflows connecting local IDEs with Jupyter/Colab kernels for production-ready code [30, 31].

## 8. Data Sources Directory & Tools

- **Yahoo Finance (`yfinance`):** Free source for daily Open, High, Low, Close, Volume (OHLCV) price data across stocks, indexes, ETFs, currencies, crypto, and bonds [31, 36, 37]. Offers up to 100 years of daily history [36]. *Note: Fundamental data is limited to the last 4 years [33, 37].*
- **FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data):** Macroeconomic time series (GDP, CPI, Fed Funds, Treasury yields) accessed via `pandas_datareader` [7, 15, 20, 39].
- **Massive.com (formerly Polygon.io):** Financial news API with a free tier allowing bulk retrieval of market news and data calls [31, 33, 39].
- **Technical Analysis Libraries:** Technical indicators calculated using Python or packages such as `ta-lib` [32].
- **SEC EDGAR & Web Scraping:** Official SEC filings for financial disclosures [40, 44]. Open web endpoints like `companiesmarketcap.com` permit quick CSV downloads of global company rankings [41].
- **Alternative & Event Data:** Earnings calendars, tokenized stock trends, ETF flow reports, and sentiment sources (Reddit, Twitter, YouTube) [33, 34, 42].
